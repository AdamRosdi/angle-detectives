Hello, Detective! 👋

An angle is formed when two straight lines meet at a point (called a vertex). Look out for these 3 main types of angles:

Square Corner

Right Angle

Exactly 90°

Looks like the letter L or the corner of a book!

Small & Sharp

Acute Angle

Smaller than 90°

Cute and small! Think of a slice of pizza or a bird's beak.

Big & Wide

Obtuse Angle

Between 90° and 180°

Wide open! Like a fan or a roof of a house.
